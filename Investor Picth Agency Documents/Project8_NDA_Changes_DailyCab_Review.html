Project 8 Advisory NDA Amendments — Founder's Evaluation
Legal & Business Protection Review for Mandate Execution
Date: 5 September 2026
Ref Document: Project8_DailyCab_NDA_Suggested_Changes.pdf (3 Sept 2026)
Reviewed For: Mr. Sanjay Thakur, Founder & MD
1
Article 1: Standard Carve-Outs to Confidential Information
Accept with 1 Tweak
Project 8 added standard exceptions (a-d) regarding public info, prior knowledge, third-party disclosure, and independent development. These are standard in 99% of corporate NDAs. Accept sub-clauses (a) to (d).
However, sub-clause (e) excludes agency "ideas, concepts & know-how". To prevent Project 8 from claiming DailyCab's shuttle route models or pooling logic as general know-how, add a brief safeguard:
DailyCab Counter-Text for 1(e): "...consists of general industry knowledge and experience of the Receiving Party without reference to, incorporation of, or reliance upon DailyCab’s proprietary route algorithms, passenger pool logic, or trade secrets."
2
Article 2: Non-Solicitation Carve-Outs & Investor Non-Circumvention
Accept + Non-Circumvention Proviso
Project 8 requests exceptions for pre-existing relationships, independent approaches, and publicly available contacts. This is fair for general hiring/networking, but in a fundraising mandate, publicly available contacts must not compromise investor introductions.
DailyCab Protective Addition: "Provided that nothing in this clause shall dilute or exempt the Receiving Party from non-circumvention obligations regarding prospective investors or strategic partners introduced to DailyCab during the mandate."
3
Article 3: Deletion of 24-Month Non-Compete & Exclusivity
Accept Deletion + Concurrent Conflict Firewall
Project 8 deleted the 24-month post-mandate non-compete. Investment advisory firms routinely reject post-mandate non-competes as they advise multiple startups. Accepting deletion of the 24-month post-term non-compete is recommended.
Crucial Protection Needed: Project 8 must NOT simultaneously advise a direct intercity shuttle competitor on DailyCab's core MP routes (Indore-Bhopal/Ujjain) during the active mandate, and "unaided memory" (3.4) must exclude source code, route profitability data, and driver payout logic.
DailyCab Counter-Clause: Add Clause 3.5: "During the active mandate term, the Receiving Party shall not provide advisory services to a direct intercity shuttle competitor operating on DailyCab's specific active routes in Madhya Pradesh."
4
Article 6: Deletion of ₹5 Lakh Fixed Penalty
Acceptable Compromise
Project 8 removed the fixed ₹5,00,000 liquidated damages penalty, replacing it with actual legally recoverable losses under Indian law. Under Sec 74 of Indian Contract Act, court-awarded damages are capped at actual proven loss anyway. Fixed penalty clauses are standard pushback items. Acceptable compromise provided DailyCab maintains rights to immediate injunctive relief (court stays against data leak) and full recovery of direct legal costs.
Founder Action Plan for Discussion / Call with Project 8
1. Green Flags to Share: Agree to Article 1(a-d) carve-outs, deletion of 24-mo post-mandate non-compete, and removal of ₹5L fixed penalty.
2. Non-Negotiable Counter-Points: (i) Concurrent route-competitor firewall during active mandate, (ii) Non-circumvention clause for introduced investors, (iii) Excluding proprietary algorithms from "unaided memory" exception.
3. Next Step: Email or share these 3 small counter-tweaks to Anish Gaonkar so legal can finalize the NDA within the day.
