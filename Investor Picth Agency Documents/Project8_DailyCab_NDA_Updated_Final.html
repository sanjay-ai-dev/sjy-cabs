ARTICLE 1 — Definition of Confidential Information & Standard Exclusions Amended
"Confidential Information" includes all business, technical, financial, and operational information disclosed by DailyCab, including intercity shuttle algorithms, route profitability models, 6-seat Ertiga pooling logic, driver payout structures, passenger databases, and subscription pass mechanisms.
Standard Exclusions: Confidential Information shall not include information which: (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of DailyCab’s Confidential Information; (d) is lawfully obtained from a third party; or (e) consists of general industry knowledge, skills, and experience of the Receiving Party without reference to, incorporation of, or reliance upon DailyCab’s proprietary route algorithms, unit economics, or trade secrets.
ARTICLE 2 — Obligations of Non-Disclosure, Non-Use & Investor Non-Circumvention Amended
The Receiving Party agrees to maintain strict confidentiality and use Confidential Information SOLELY for the purpose of evaluating and executing the potential advisory mandate between the Parties. If the Parties do not enter into a mandate or if the association terminates, the Receiving Party shall NOT use, directly or indirectly, any Confidential Information or business playbooks to launch, advise, or invest in a competing intercity shuttle service.
Non-Solicitation & Non-Circumvention: General recruitment qualifications apply to pre-existing contacts or public job postings; provided that nothing in this clause shall dilute or exempt the Receiving Party from non-circumvention obligations regarding prospective investors or partners specifically introduced during the mandate.
ARTICLE 3 — Advisory Capacity & Active Mandate Competitor Restrictions Amended
3.1 The Receiving Party acts in an independent advisory capacity across mobility sectors and is not subject to a blanket post-termination non-compete.
3.2 Concurrent Active-Term Firewall: During the active term of the engagement, the Receiving Party shall not provide advisory services to a direct intercity shuttle competitor operating on DailyCab's active routes in Madhya Pradesh (e.g. Indore-Bhopal, Indore-Ujjain).
3.3 Unaided Memory Limitation: Clause 3.4 permitting general industry experience retained in unaided memory shall EXCLUDE DailyCab’s proprietary algorithms, route profitability metrics, financial models, and driver payout logic.
ARTICLE 4 — Intellectual Property Assignment
All custom work product, investor presentations, and strategic deliverables created specifically for DailyCab during the engagement shall belong exclusively to DailyCab upon full payment of agreed advisory fees.
ARTICLE 5 — Term & Survival
This Agreement shall take effect upon execution. In the event negotiations terminate or the mandate expires, all confidentiality and non-use covenants shall survive for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure.
ARTICLE 6 — Remedies & Legally Recoverable Losses Amended
In the event of a material breach of confidentiality or non-use, the aggrieved Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction. Monetary compensation shall be limited to actual direct losses legally recoverable under applicable Indian law, along with reasonable legal expenses incurred in enforcing this Agreement.
ARTICLE 7 — Governing Law & Exclusive Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India (Indian Contract Act, 1872 & Information Technology Act, 2000). Disputes arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent Courts at Indore / Dhar, Madhya Pradesh.
